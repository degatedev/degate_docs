# Quickstart

Install the app and add funds to start trading on-chain stocks or opening stock LP positions in Turbo Range. The steps below cover wallet setup and a first trade.

## 1. Install the app

Use the iOS or Android app for transactions. Download it here:

[![Download on the App Store](https://hub.degate.com/common/wallet-setup-img-en/Download_AppStore.png)](https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6742168343?pt=127643354\&ct=docs\&mt=8) [![Get it on Google Play](https://hub.degate.com/common/wallet-setup-img-en/Download_GooglePlay.png)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.app.degate\&referrer=utm_source%3Ddocs%26utm_medium%3Ddocs) [![Download APK](https://hub.degate.com/common/wallet-setup-img-en/Download_Android.png)](https://download.degate.com/apps/degate-android.apk)

## 2. Create your wallet

Create a new wallet with a 12-word recovery phrase (**Create wallet**), import or connect one you already have (**Import wallet** / **Connect external wallet**), or choose **Continue with email**. We do not recommend keeping large balances in an email wallet. For higher-value assets, use a recovery phrase wallet or a supported hardware wallet and keep its backup securely. Full walkthrough: [Create a Wallet](create-a-wallet.md).

## 3. Fund your wallet

Tap **Receive**, copy your address for the chain you are sending from (or scan the QR code), and send. Your DeGate address works across all EVM chains; Solana, Aptos, and Bitcoin have their own address formats. Supported assets appear in your balance, but remain recorded on their respective chains. Check the receiving network as well as the address before sending.


## 4. Make your first swap

Open **Trade**, choose a supported stock token or another token, and enter the amount to buy with USDC. Review the quote, expected output, network, and costs before signing. A hardware account needs the relevant native gas token at its own address. Step-by-step guide: [Your First Swap](your-first-swap.md).

To open a stock LP instead, select a pool in [Turbo Range](../features/turbo-range.md). A USDC-only deposit prepares the stock-token side during opening; buying the token first is not required.
