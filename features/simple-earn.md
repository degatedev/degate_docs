# Simple Earn

Use Simple Earn to deposit USDC or SOL into available DeFi vaults. There is no DeGate deposit, withdrawal, or performance fee. The vault protocol determines how deposits are used and how returns are generated.

* **Underlying protocols.** Available vaults use Morpho on Ethereum and Kamino on Solana. The live vault list is in the app.
* **Cross-chain deposits.** On supported routes, your USDC is sent to the selected vault's network. Check the destination and quote before depositing. On Solana you can also deposit SOL (via the Kamino Jito Market).
* **Position management.** View deposits and available actions in the app. Review the current value, estimated yield, and withdrawal conditions for the vault you select.

## What returns to expect

Check the selected vault for its current estimated APY. The estimate can change with lending demand, liquidity, and the underlying strategy; it does not guarantee future returns. Review the protocol, deposit asset, withdrawal conditions, and risks before signing.

## FAQ

**Where does the yield come from?**
The selected Morpho or Kamino vault strategy generates yield through the underlying protocol's lending markets. Use Simple Earn to deposit and manage the position.

**What does Simple Earn cost?**
There is no DeGate deposit, withdrawal, or performance fee. Network costs and the underlying protocol's terms still apply.

**Can I withdraw at any time?**
There is no additional DeGate lock-up period. Check the selected vault's withdrawal conditions and available liquidity before depositing.

**Do I give up custody when I deposit?**
Deposits go into the selected vault's smart contracts, authorized by your key. The vault contracts govern your deposit and its risks; Simple Earn provides the interface for managing it.
