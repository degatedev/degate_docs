# FAQ

Find answers about on-chain stock trading, Turbo Range, fees, and wallet recovery. Follow the links for detailed steps.

## Product

**What is DeGate in one sentence?**
DeGate is a self-custody multichain crypto wallet for trading on-chain stocks and managing stock LP positions through Turbo Range. More: [What is DeGate](../README.md).

**Is DeGate a DEX?**
No. The earlier order book DEX closed in June 2025. The current product is a self-custody multichain crypto wallet with on-chain stock trading and stock LP management. More: [From DEX to Self-Custody Wallet](../about-degate/from-dex-to-self-custody-wallet.md).

**Which chains are supported?**
DeGate supports **16+ mainnet blockchains**, including Ethereum, Solana, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon, BNB Smart Chain, Avalanche, Aptos, Bitcoin, Linea, Sonic, Worldchain, xLayer, HyperEVM, Monad, and Robinhood Chain. More: [Supported networks](../README.md#supported-networks).

**Is there a desktop or web version?**
The web app at [app.degate.com](https://app.degate.com) does not support transactions. Use the **iOS or Android mobile app** for swaps, transfers, and Turbo Range position actions.

**What languages does the app support?**
The app supports 13 languages, including English, Deutsch, Français, Español, Italiano, 日本語, 한국어, and Chinese (simplified and traditional). The interface follows your device language settings.

**Can I connect a hardware wallet?**
Yes. Connect a Bluetooth-capable OneKey or Ledger model in the DeGate mobile app. Check the connection screen for supported models and networks. More: [Create a Wallet](../getting-started/create-a-wallet.md).

## Money

**What are the fees?**
Ordinary swaps have two platform fee tiers, based on the token traded against USDC: 0% for stablecoins, 0.02% for all other tokens, as of August 2026. Supported USDC bridging and Simple Earn have no DeGate platform fee. Automatic swaps within Turbo Range have no DeGate platform swap fee; network costs and pool trading fees may still apply. More: [Fees](../fees.md).

**Can I buy crypto with a card?**
Card purchases are not currently available in DeGate. Fund your wallet by depositing crypto from any supported chain; see [Quickstart](../getting-started/quickstart.md).

**Does using DeGate require identity verification?**
You can create a recovery phrase wallet without uploading identity documents or completing exchange-style identity verification. Email sign-in has its own setup, including email verification and a DeGate password. Third-party services may have separate identity and regional requirements; see [Create a Wallet](../getting-started/create-a-wallet.md).

## Troubleshooting

**My swap is taking longer than expected.**
Cross-chain swaps typically complete within 30 seconds to 2 minutes; the app shows an estimated completion time before you confirm. Check the status in the **Activity** tab; network congestion can occasionally cause delays. If it fails or remains pending, check its status and transaction record before trying again. Send us the transaction hash through Feedback if you need help.

**My hardware wallet transaction failed for lack of gas.**
Hardware wallet accounts pay network gas from their own address in the chain's native token, not from your DeGate balance. Add a small amount of that chain's native token (for example, ETH on Base) and retry. Details: [Fees](../fees.md).

**My balance shows zero after a deposit.**
Deposits can take a few minutes depending on the blockchain's confirmation time. Pull down to refresh. If the balance is still missing after 10 minutes, check the transaction on a block explorer (Etherscan, Solscan) to confirm it was sent to the correct address on the correct network, then contact support.

## Trust

**Who holds my keys?**
Key handling and recovery depend on the wallet type. Recovery phrase and hardware wallets differ from email and external-wallet / web-sync accounts.
